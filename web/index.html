Give unused parts a second life.

E Xchange is a smart exchange marketplace for used electronic and mechanical components. It intelligently connects people who need a specific part with people who already have one sitting unused.

How It Works Explore the Idea

This page presents a hackathon prototype and concept. E Xchange is not a live marketplace, and nothing here is a real listing, user, or transaction.

The part you need is probably sitting in someone's drawer.

Unused motherboards, computer parts, electronics, appliances, and mechanical components pile up in homes and workshops. Meanwhile, someone else may desperately need that exact part.

Electronic and mechanical waste

Working parts get thrown out or stored away because there is no easy way to pass them on.

Expensive replacement parts

Buying new can cost more than a repair is worth, so people end up replacing whole devices.

Hard-to-find components

Specific models and older parts are difficult to track down on general marketplaces.

Little trust in used parts

Buyers can't tell whether a used part works, and honest sellers can't easily prove that it does.

A smart exchange between the part and the person who needs it.

E Xchange is an intelligent platform that brings supply and demand together, and takes care of the awkward steps in between.

Matches demand with available components

Requests and listings are compared so the right part finds the right person.

Analyzes listings and suggests prices

Each listing is evaluated and a fair price is suggested, so sellers and buyers start from the same number.

Helps verify and test components

Parts can be inspected or tested so buyers know what they are getting.

Facilitates secure transactions

Payment is coordinated through the platform instead of a leap of faith between strangers.

Coordinates delivery

Handover or shipping is arranged so the part actually reaches the buyer.

How E Xchange works

Five steps from a forgotten part to a working one in someone else's hands.

  1. 1

    List

    Someone lists a component, describing what it is and what condition it is in.

  2. 2

    Analyze

    E Xchange evaluates the item and suggests a fair price.

  3. 3

    Match

    The system finds someone who is looking for that component.

  4. 4

    Verify

    The component can be tested or inspected before the exchange is completed.

  5. 5

    Exchange

    Payment and delivery are facilitated for both sides.

Every part reused is one less thing wasted.

A part that works should end up where it is useful. E Xchange is built around that simple idea.

  • Extends the life of electronics and mechanical components

    Parts keep working in a second home instead of ending their life early.

  • Reduces unnecessary waste

    Fewer working components are discarded, and fewer devices are replaced outright.

  • Makes repairs more affordable

    A matching used part can cost far less than a brand-new replacement.

  • Creates value from unused equipment

    What sits in a drawer becomes something another person is glad to find.

  • Builds a trusted circular marketplace

    Testing, fair pricing, and secure handling make buying used feel safe.

Starting with electronics. Built to grow.

The same idea works anywhere a specific used part sits idle while someone else needs it. E Xchange could eventually expand into these areas.

Computer components

Motherboards, memory, storage, power supplies, and other parts for building and repairing computers.

Industrial equipment

Spare parts and machinery components from workshops, plants, and job sites.

Automotive parts

Used vehicle parts that keep cars, trucks, and motorbikes on the road.

Electrical components

Switches, motors, controllers, and other electrical parts for repairs and projects.

Mechanical parts

Gears, bearings, fittings, and other mechanical components that are hard to source individually.

Future direction only. The current prototype focuses on the core exchange idea.
