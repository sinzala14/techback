Section 2 • The Problem

Four interconnected challenges in the electronics market.

Technology is essential for education, business operations, communication, and economic participation—yet acquiring, maintaining, and recovering value from devices remains broken.

2.1 Cost Barrier

The High Cost of Technology

Students and young professionals need laptops and smartphones for academic and career development, but face steep upfront capital requirements—even when they can afford manageable periodic payments.

2.2 Operational Risk

Business Technology Downtime

Small and medium-sized businesses depend on computers, printers, and networking equipment, yet lack structured maintenance and asset management. Reactive repairs are expensive, unpredictable, and halt operations.

2.3 Unrecovered Value

Electronic Waste & Lost Value

Thousands of damaged, outdated, or unneeded devices sit idle or get discarded despite containing valuable spare components or being fully repairable for productive reuse.

2.4 Disconnected Market

Fragmented Electronics Services

Device sellers, repair technicians, refurbishment shops, and recycling businesses operate in silos—making it difficult to coordinate acquisition, maintenance, resale, and disposal in one trusted ecosystem.

New Feature • Save-to-Own

Overcoming upfront barriers with 0% interest progressive ownership.

Save-to-Own by eXchange Limited enables students, freelancers, and small businesses to acquire essential laptops and phones through weekly mobile money milestones—turning e-waste into deposit discounts.

Step 1: Token Build

Build Qualification Tokens

Hand in damaged screens, dead phone batteries, or old PC scrap at campus drop-off kiosks. Each item credits Green Tokens to your account (e.g. 50–120 tokens qualifies you for a laptop).

Step 2: 0% Layaway Lock

Lock Device with Milestone Savings

Select your certified laptop or smartphone, lock the purchase price over 4, 8, or 12 weeks, and redeem Green Tokens as a direct reduction on your initial lock-in deposit (zero interest, zero hidden debt).

Step 3: Verified Handover

Take Ownership + e-TechCard

Complete milestones via MTN/Airtel/Zamtel Mobile Money. Receive your inspected device with a 6-month warranty and its unique digital e-TechCard passport already registered.

Core Innovation • e-TechCard

The digital lifecycle identity and service passport for every device.

Developed under eXchange Limited, the e-TechCard is a permanent, cryptographic digital record linked to a physical device’s serial number and QR code—protecting buyers, technicians, and businesses.

Cryptographic Device Identity

Full Provenance, Diagnostic History & Anti-Theft Verification

Every device sold, serviced, or traded through TechBack is issued a digital e-TechCard. Whether a student is buying a refurbished Dell laptop or a business is tracking 50 office workstations, the card tracks condition grading, authenticated repairs, genuine parts used, and verified ownership transfers.

  • Immutable Service History: Logs technician notes, test results, and battery cycle health.
  • Anti-Theft Protection: Proof-of-ownership certificate tied to user NRC/Student ID.
  • Higher Resale Value: Verified provenance commands up to 25% higher resale and trade-in value.
  • End-of-Life Tracking: Guides device to harvesting or certified e-waste recycling when retired.
